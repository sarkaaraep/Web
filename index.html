Counselling Mission Plan

TNEA is run online by Anna University on behalf of the Government of Tamil Nadu.

1
Register Online

Create an account on the official TNEA portal, pay the registration fee and fill in personal and academic details.

2
Certificate Verification

Upload documents; verification happens at a TFC centre or online as notified. Rank list is then published.

3
Choice Filling

List colleges and branches in your order of preference. Fill many choices; order matters most.

4
Choice Locking & Allotment

Lock your choices. Seats are allotted by rank, community and your priority order.

5
Accept / Upward / Decline

Accept & Join, Accept & Upward (try for a better seat in the next round), or Decline. Then report to the college with fees and documents.

6
Rounds

Typically 3 rounds plus supplementary rounds for the General Academic stream, with a separate Vocational stream round.

Key Information

Always confirm exact dates, fees and rules on the official TNEA site (tneaonline.org) and the current information brochure.

Cutoff Formula

Out of 200: Maths ÷ 2 + Physics ÷ 4 + Chemistry ÷ 4 (marks out of 100 each). Ties are broken using Maths, Physics and other rules in the brochure.

Eligibility

Passed Class 12 (HSC) with Physics, Chemistry, Maths and the other subject criteria in the brochure. Vocational stream students have a separate list.

Communities

OC, BC, BCM, MBC/DNC, SC, SCA, ST. Each has its own rank list and reservation share.

7.5% Reservation

Government school students get a special preferential reservation in professional courses, with its own list.

Documents

  • Class 10 & 12 marksheets
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Community certificate
  • Aadhaar, photo, first-graduate/special category proofs if any

Pro Tips

  • Choose branch + college balance
  • Check NBA/NAAC, placements, labs
  • Never leave choices unlocked
  • Keep 50+ choices ready

Courses & Branches

Popular B.E./B.Tech options through TNEA.

CSE / IT / AI&DS

Software, AI, data science, cloud. Highest demand, highest cutoffs.

ECE / EEE

Electronics, VLSI, embedded, power systems, EV and core-sector jobs.

Mechanical / Mechatronics

Design, manufacturing, automotive, robotics, automation.

Civil / Architecture

Infrastructure, construction, structural and environmental work.

Chemical / Biotech / Food Tech

Process industries, pharma, bio-engineering, food processing.

Agri / Textile / Others

Niche branches with good scope and easier cutoffs.
